# Story 36A — Explain the compact GDP historical bands

## User story

As a reader, I want concise help for the compact Real GDP chart so that I can understand its historical bands without opening the full research view.

## Completed behavior

- A help button appears at the compact chart’s lower-right corner.
- The compact line shows the latest 20 quarters, or five years.
- A small muted title below the chart reports the line’s actual first and last quarters.
- Click, tap, Enter, or Space opens an anchored explanation.
- Escape closes the explanation and restores focus to the button.
- Clicking or tapping outside the chart closes the explanation.
- The text explains the comparison period, middle 50%, middle 80%, and readings in the outer 10% tails.
- The explanation uses 14px text and remains constrained to the narrow viewport.

The chart bands and full GDP research view are unchanged.
